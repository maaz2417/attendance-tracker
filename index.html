Purpose

An easy-to-use university attendance tracker for recording student attendance by subject and teacher, then showing useful summaries before attendance becomes a problem.

Important Screens

  • Dashboard: today's classes
  • Subjects: subjects grouped by teacher and semester.
  • Attendance session: fast attendance marking with a clear save action.
  • Student detail: attendance history, percentage, absences.

Useful Rules

  • Attendance percentage = attended sessions / total sessions, with Late counted as attended unless the administrator changes the rule.
  • Excused absences should be visible separately from unexcused absences.
  • The app should warn students when their attendance drops below a threshold, such as 75%.

Nice Additions Later

  • CSV or PDF report export.
  • Import students from a spreadsheet.
  • QR code check-in for faster attendance.
  • Notifications for low attendance or missed classes.
  • Calendar view for scheduled classes.
  • Role-based login and permissions.
  • Offline support with sync when the connection returns.
